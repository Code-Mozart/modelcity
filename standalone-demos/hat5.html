Through-node curves




  node (drag)
  arrow tip: heading
  blue: where the two heading lines meet (drag: sets both headings); on an S-curve the inflection point, generated
  turquoise: corners of the control polygon (S-curve: where the heading lines meet the middle straight)
  orange ring: radius (drag; headings stay; both arcs of an S-curve share it; double-click resets to the largest that fits)
  Right-click or Del removes a node. Esc returns to Move.
